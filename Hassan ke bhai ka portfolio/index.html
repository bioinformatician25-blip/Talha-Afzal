Research focus

Aqua CultureGenomics EcologyBehaviour

I'm a Lecturer in Bio-sciences with the Higher Education Department, Azad Jammu & Kashmir, teaching undergraduate courses spanning Animal Life, Genetics, Ecology, Parasitology, Bacteriology & Virology, and Aqua Culture since 2021.

My research sits at the intersection of Aqua Culture biology, genomics and bioinformatics — from reproductive biology and captive-breeding of Rainbow Trout to histopathology, animal behaviour, and applying computational and AI/ML approaches to biological data. I hold an MS (Hons) in Bio-sciences from the University of Poonch, Rawalakot.

  • ◆ Aqua Culture biology & aquaculture (Rainbow Trout reproduction and rearing)
  • ◆ Genomics, NGS transcriptomics & bioinformatic analysis
  • ◆ Animal behaviour, ecology & histopathology
